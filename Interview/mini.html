★ do you use a planner/schedule?

none at all.

★ why not?

i cannot keep up maintaining a planner or schedule, i usually remember everything that i need to get done.

sometimes i wish i do plan or schedule like for things in advance such as events, but i do set reminders for events that are really far away in the future.

and sometimes i use my mom as a planner, to help remind me of things.

★ have you ever tried to use a planner or keep a calendar before?

i've tried to-do-lists before and i do not like them. i prefer reminders and generally, if it's something important, i would remember it already. it's never been a problem for me to not have a planner.

my homework is already organized on canvas, and i remember my deadlines due to the pressure and obligation of having to complete them on time motivating me. i would think back to the classes and would remember the homework like that.

although, it's not so easy to remember hangouts because sometimes i would agree to one and then realize after that my schedule clashes with it. also events in the city.

during high school, my mom also encouraged me to keep a planner or at least write to-do-lists - which is when i discovered i did not like them. she would tell me to just write all my tasks down and that i do not have to complete it by the end of the day, it's just to keep me organized and reminded.

★ do you just not enjoy planning?

i'm a spontaneous person and "not a planned person", so i prefer to not make schedules as i can't follow them especially if it's about work that needs to be done in the day like errands, work - especially when it's scheduled to the hour like 4 to 5 PM to do homework or do laundry. if it's scheduled like that, i probably won't want to do it when it does become 4 to 5 PM.

i'll only ever "schedule" if it's like meetings or something adjacent to that.

i usually plan the things i want to do in my head after i've woken up, and i do things based on what i feel like doing today or at that moment. i just naturally plan or think about what i want to do and what needs to be done in the beginning of the day, especially on days that i am more free.

during the day, i like keeping some times throughout free so that there is room for spontaneity.

my mom instilled the habit of planning in the morning, but i generally dislike the rigid process of thinking about schedules and putting it down onto paper or a screen.

sometimes i use my notes app to write down reminders or things i think i'm going to forget later, but it's not something i constantly update.
